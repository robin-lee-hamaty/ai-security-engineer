# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a welcoming, respectful space for everyone learning and teaching AI security, regardless of experience level or background. We want beginners and experts alike to feel able to ask questions, propose corrections, and disagree constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and professional.
- Assume good faith and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Keep discussion technical and on-topic.
- Credit others' work and sources.
- Remember that people are learning; answer questions generously.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or demeaning language.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.
- Encouraging or providing help with unauthorized, illegal, or harmful activity.
- Posting secrets, credentials, malware, stolen data, or unauthorized-access material.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project communication — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Violations may be addressed by editing, removing, or rejecting contributions, and by blocking repeat or serious offenders. Concerns can be raised by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer privately through the repository profile.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from widely used open-source community standards, kept intentionally concise for an educational project.
