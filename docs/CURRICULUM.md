# Curriculum Map

*AI Security Engineer* progresses in ordered stages. The groups below summarize the book's actual coverage; they are a map, not a replacement for the book.

## 1. Foundations
Computing, files and permissions, networking, HTTP and TLS; Python essentials; JSON and structured data; authentication vs authorization; machine-learning foundations; language-model and transformer foundations; tokenization, embeddings and cosine similarity; logits, softmax, temperature and top-p; Retrieval-Augmented Generation; fine-tuning vs RAG; agents; a beginner defensive lab and a run-the-code primer.

## 2. Core AI Security
AI security as system security; assets, threat actors and security properties; trust boundaries; defense-in-depth; model risk vs application risk; direct and indirect prompt injection; jailbreaks and guardrail engineering.

## 3. Agentic & RAG Security
LLM trust boundaries and agentic systems; agent identity, memory, multi-agent and control planes; tool and function abuse; RAG security; data leakage and exfiltration.

## 4. Advanced Attack Surface
Model-layer and adversarial machine learning; AI supply chain, MLOps and plugin/MCP security; multimodal, browser and computer-use security.

## 5. Architecture & Engineering
AI application threat modelling; secure AI SDLC; architecture and platform engineering for security; secure deployment and production hardening.

## 6. Red Teaming & Operations
Evaluation, red teaming and safety testing; AI security operations, detection, incident response and forensics.

## 7. Assessment & Reporting
Professional AI security assessment methodology; technical report writing and communication for engineers, managers, executives and auditors.

## 8. Governance & Management
Frameworks, governance, privacy and compliance; executive analysis and board reporting; security program management and operating model; metrics, maturity and budgeting.

## 9. Practice & Evaluation
Safe laboratory design; content and learning engineering; hands-on practice labs; assessments and examinations with answer keys; a case-study library; a master glossary.

## 10. Research & Academic Development
University and research extension: mathematical foundations, transformers and deep learning; cloud, Kubernetes and model-serving security; research methods; privacy engineering; ethics; formal assurance; and professor-level teaching and research-supervision practice.

For the definitive structure, read the book's own Preface and Curriculum Map and Table of Contents.
