# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve *AI Security Engineer*. This is a free educational resource, and it gets better when readers test, challenge, and correct it.

## What is welcome

- **Factual corrections** — anything technically inaccurate or outdated.
- **Broken-code reports** — an example that does not run, or whose output does not match.
- **Reference updates** — newer primary sources (NIST, OWASP, MITRE, ISO, peer-reviewed papers) or replacements for dead links.
- **Teaching improvements** — clearer explanations, better worked examples, additional exercises.
- **Typo and formatting fixes.**
- **Defensive-security enhancements** — better detection, mitigation, or safe-lab design.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue describing the problem or proposal, with the section or page reference.
2. For text or reference changes, describe the change clearly. For code, include the corrected snippet and its real output.
3. Keep contributions accurate and verifiable. Cite primary sources where relevant.

## What must NOT be submitted

- stolen data, credentials, secrets, tokens, or private information;
- malware or live exploit payloads;
- material describing or enabling unauthorized access to systems;
- third-party copyrighted books or content.

## Style

- Keep a professional, educational tone.
- Prefer defensive framing: explain the attack to defend against it.
- Code should be runnable and its output real.
